Picking, packing & shipping

Turn order work into a shipment workflow.

Explore outbound planning, floor execution, and shipment follow-up in TK WMS.

Organize demand into work

Order menus, wave planning, and allocation cover the planning side of fulfillment. These stages establish the context for picking work. Which options users can apply depends on configured processes and permissions rather than on the public product website.

Follow physical fulfillment

Picking leads into packing verification and cartonization in supported workflows. Shipment grouping and audits provide additional stages before carrier shipping. TK WMS describes these as separate operational capabilities, allowing teams to review the part of the process relevant to their work.

Review shipment activity

Shipment queries provide lookup paths after or during fulfillment review. Picking, Allocation, Consolidation, and Picking Performance dashboards offer broader functional views. Use an inquiry for a specific record and a dashboard for a wider view of an outbound area.
