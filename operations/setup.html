Warehouse & item setup

Build the reference structure for warehouse work.

Maintain the master data and location context used throughout TK WMS operations.

Define items and handling context

Item setup covers SKU descriptions, categories, and units of measure. LPN and carton definitions describe handling context. These records provide reference information for operational work rather than representing an individual receipt or order.

Organize the warehouse layout

Locations, aisles, zones, layouts, and pick routes describe the warehouse structure. Suggested Location Rules belong to a separate configuration area that can use location context in supported workflows. Defining a location and configuring a location suggestion are different tasks.

Maintain trading and shipping references

Supplier, customer, and carrier records provide additional master-data context. Warehouse and business-unit administration establishes the broader setup. Users need the appropriate access for maintenance, with available menus governed by their role and configuration.
