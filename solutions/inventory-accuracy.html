Inventory accuracy

Know what is recorded. Know what needs review.

Bring stock visibility, count work, and traceability into a connected inventory-control approach.

When the physical count disagrees

Begin by establishing the warehouse, item, location, and handling unit involved. A quantity difference can be difficult to interpret without that context. TK WMS’s Inventory Workbench provides the recorded view; cycle count work provides the physical verification path.

Before treating on-hand as available

On-hand and allocated quantities answer different questions. LPN holds add status context, while reservation attributes belong to stock-selection configuration. Review the recorded quantity, commitment, and relevant status before deciding that a stock figure represents usable inventory.

A review example: one location, two questions

Imagine a location where the physical count differs from the recorded quantity. Count plans and tasks organize verification; variance review addresses the difference. If the quantity agrees but the handling unit is held, the investigation concerns status instead. These are illustrative situations that lead to different TK WMS tools.

Keep the next action specific

Use an adjustment for an authorized record correction, an LPN move for a location change, and a hold or release workflow for status handling. Serial and recall inquiries serve traceability questions. Choosing an action follows the investigation rather than replacing it.
