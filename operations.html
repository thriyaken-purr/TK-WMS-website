TK WMS / OPERATIONS

Explore the work that runs your warehouse.

Understand the day-to-day workspaces in TK WMS, from master data and arrival processing to production work and outbound fulfillment.

Execution has distinct stages

Receiving a delivery, moving an LPN, reviewing a count difference, and packing an order are different tasks. TK WMS represents these through operational areas with their own records, status views, and actions. Each guide below focuses on one area rather than sending you through the full product overview.

Follow work in its warehouse context

Warehouse and item setup establish reference information. Inbound brings goods into the operation; inventory and replenishment support stock handling; outbound organizes demand through shipment. Kitting, counting, and cross-docking provide dedicated paths for specialized work.
