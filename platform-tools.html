TK WMS / Strategy & reservations

Strategy & reservations

TK WMS places strategy configuration beside day-to-day execution. These menus are for implementation and operations teams maintaining how the system should make configured warehouse decisions—not for viewing a live transaction queue.

Strategy Engine

Maintain strategies used by configured warehouse processes. The menu provides a central place to work with strategy definitions rather than hard-coding every decision into a screen.

Strategy actions

Review the actions associated with strategy and rule behavior. Actions define what the system is configured to do when a decision path applies.

Suggested Location Rules

Configure the rules used to guide suggested warehouse locations in supported workflows, connecting location setup with the decision logic used during execution.

Reservation Attribute Profiles

Define attribute profiles used by inventory reservation controls, adding a configurable layer to how stock can be selected for warehouse demand.

SEPARATE WHERE STOCK GOES FROM WHAT GETS RESERVED

Suggested Location Rules guide location suggestions in supported workflows. Reservation Attribute Profiles address attributes used by reservation logic. They configure different decisions and are maintained in their own areas.

TK WMS / Business rules & policies

Business rules & policies

Rules & Profiles collects rule setup, actions, workflow policies, and reservation-related profiles. It is a configuration surface for authorized teams; the public guide explains the menu purpose without exposing rule values or customer configuration.

Business Rules Assistant setup

Open the assistant setup area associated with business-rule configuration and the policies that guide warehouse processes.

Rule Actions

Maintain the actions linked to configured business rules, providing the operational outcome side of rule-driven behavior.

Workflow Policies

Review and configure policies associated with workflow execution, keeping policy setup distinct from the screens and menus operators use.

Profiles & attributes

Manage reservation-attribute profiles used by reservation logic, in conjunction with strategy and inventory configuration.

MAINTAIN POLICY ALONGSIDE EXECUTION

Authorized configuration teams maintain business-rule setup, Rule Actions, and Workflow Policies. These menus define configured behavior; they are separate from transaction queues and operator work screens.

TK WMS / Workflow & RF/voice

Workflow & RF/voice

The system exposes designers and menu setup for configuring the experience around warehouse tasks. These are implementation tools alongside the workflows they help present.

Screen Designer

Configure the screens used in supported warehouse workflows, including the information presented as users perform their tasks.

Workflow Designer & Policies

Work with workflow structure and its policies so process steps and the rules governing them can be managed in the configuration area.

RF Menus

Maintain radio-frequency menu paths used by warehouse workers on handheld devices, separate from the public product site's navigation.

Voice configuration

Review voice-related options for guided warehouse tasks as part of the configurable worker experience.

SHAPE THE WORKER-FACING PROCESS

Use Screen Designer for supported screen configuration, Workflow Designer for process structure, and RF Menus for handheld menu paths. Workflow policies and voice-related options are separate configuration surfaces.

TK WMS / Dashboards & queries

Dashboards & queries

Dashboards summarize work by warehouse function; queries provide lookup paths into operational records and history. Together they help users move from an area-level view to a specific shipment, receipt, transaction, serial, or user activity inquiry.

Named dashboard views

Menu views include Putaway, Picking, Allocation, Replenishment, Inbound Performance, Task Activity, Consolidation, and Picking Performance dashboards.

Inbound & putaway

Follow inbound performance and putaway work, including receipts waiting for their next warehouse action.

Picking, allocation & consolidation

Review work at the planning and fulfillment stages, including picking performance and consolidation activity.

Queries & traceability

Use shipment, inventory, receipt, and transaction queries, plus serial-tracking and user-activity inquiries to investigate specific history.

MOVE FROM A SUMMARY TO AN INQUIRY

Use a functional dashboard to review an area such as putaway or picking. When an individual record needs investigation, use the relevant receipt, shipment, inventory, transaction, serial, or user-activity inquiry.

TK WMS / Tools, access & training

Tools, access & training

These menus serve implementation, integration, administration, and training tasks around the operational WMS. They include both design-time tools and access/setup controls.

Report, Label & Interface Designers

Use separate designer menus to maintain report layouts, warehouse labels, and system-interface definitions.

Integration Sync & data utilities

Review integration synchronization, use import/export utilities for supported data workflows, and explore the API playground for API-oriented work.

Warehouse, user & access administration

Configure warehouse and business-unit records, user accounts, roles, access permissions, and the menus presented to users.

Training Phases 1–6

Follow the six phased training areas exposed in TK WMS to organize product learning by stage.

SUPPORT CONFIGURATION AND PRODUCT LEARNING

Report, Label, and Interface Designers support different implementation tasks. Integration Sync and import/export cover supported data work, while warehouse and role setup control context and access. Training Phases 1–6 organize learning by stage.

Feature availability depends on your role and warehouse configuration.
