TK WMS platform

One platform across the warehouse lifecycle.

TK WMS brings execution, configurable decision tools, and operational visibility together. Select an area in Explore for more detail about its menus.

01 / FOUNDATION

Warehouse setup & master data

Build the reference structure used by warehouse work: SKU descriptions, categories and units of measure; locations, aisles, zones and layouts; LPN and carton definitions; suppliers, customers, carriers; and pick-route setup.

02 / INBOUND

Receiving & putaway

Follow arrivals through appointments and yard activity into receipt processing, returns and inspection, label generation, and putaway. The inbound views include appointment and receipt status so teams can see work waiting at the receiving stage.

03 / INVENTORY

Control & accuracy

Use the inventory workbench to review stock by warehouse and location, then move or adjust LPNs, place or release holds, run cycle counts, and investigate serial or recall activity.

04 / OUTBOUND

Order fulfillment

Move demand through wave planning, allocation, picking, packing verification, and cartonization. Continue through shipment grouping, audits, and carrier shipping, with query menus for shipment follow-up.

05 / VALUE-ADDED

Kitting & cross-docking

Manage kitting bills of material, assembly or disassembly work orders, work stations, and production progress. Cross-dock work has a dedicated workbench for coordinating eligible inbound goods with outbound demand.

06 / CONFIGURATION

Rules, strategies & workflows

Configure how work is directed with the Strategy Engine, rule actions, suggested-location rules, reservation-attribute profiles, workflow policies, screen designers, and RF/voice menu tools.

A connected warehouse operation

The whole operation.
Not just the next task.

TK WMS spans the work visible across its menus, from master data and inbound execution through inventory control, outbound fulfillment, and value-added operations.

Master data & warehouse foundation01 / 13

Set up the structure work depends on.

These are the reference records used by receiving, inventory, picking, and shipping. The menus cover item definitions and warehouse geography, plus the LPNs, packaging, partners, and routes that the execution screens refer back to.

Item Master

Maintain each SKU's description, category, unit of measure, status, and other item attributes used in warehouse workflows. The item catalog also brings on-hand, allocated, and primary-location information into view for item review.

Locations, aisles, zones & layout

Define the warehouse's addressable structure and group locations into aisles and zones. These records provide the location context used by inventory inquiries, putaway, picking, and other work.

LPNs, cartons & trading partners

Maintain license-plate records and carton definitions, along with supplier, customer, and carrier records referenced by inbound and outbound activity.

Pick-route setup

Maintain warehouse pick routes as configuration records. Route setup sits alongside the physical location structure that picking work must navigate.

Inbound operations02 / 13

Coordinate what arrives and where it goes.

Inbound spans expected arrivals, dock-side work, receiving, inspection, and final placement. The dashboards and work queues help distinguish scheduled work from receipts or putaway tasks that still need attention.

Appointments & yard

Review inbound appointments, arrivals, and yard activity. The inbound dashboard surfaces appointments due and arrivals at the dock so teams can monitor the receiving queue.

Receiving & ASN check-in

Work through receipt screens, including purchase-order receipt and ASN check-in paths observed in TK WMS, then follow a receipt into subsequent warehouse work.

Returns & quality inspection

Keep return processing and inspection work distinct from ordinary receiving. Inspection gives teams a review step before returned or received goods continue through their configured flow.

Labels & putaway

Generate warehouse labels and track receipts awaiting putaway. Putaway dashboards show pending work and help teams follow goods from the dock toward a storage location.

Outbound operations03 / 13

Move orders from release to shipment.

Outbound connects demand planning to floor execution and shipping. The menu set includes both planning controls and the individual work stages that follow an order after it is released.

Orders & wave planning

Review outbound orders, build waves from eligible work, and use the Wave Planning controls for planning criteria and overrides. The observed flow stages allocation, route, and release actions before work enters execution.

Allocation

Assign inventory to order demand as part of the wave and fulfillment flow. Allocation dashboards and inquiries provide a way to monitor that planning stage alongside released work.

Picking, packing & cartonization

Coordinate pick tasks and routes, then move orders into pack verification and cartonization. Consolidation and picking views help monitor work across these related steps.

Shipment grouping, audit & carrier shipping

Group shipments, run shipping audits, and process carrier shipping. Shipment queries provide a separate way to look up shipment activity after fulfillment work advances.

Inventory control04 / 13

Understand and control stock at the unit level.

Inventory controls combine a warehouse workbench with focused actions and traceability inquiries. Teams can review where stock sits, how much is allocated, and whether an LPN is available, held, or awaiting follow-up.

Inventory workbench

Review stock by warehouse, item, and location, including on-hand and allocated quantities. Workbench filters include held and zero-quantity inventory, with multi-select actions for supported inventory work.

Adjustments & LPN moves

Use adjustment workflows to correct inventory records and LPN move workflows to change where a handling unit is stored. These actions are separate from read-only inventory inquiries.

Holds & reservation attributes

Place or release LPN holds and review reservation controls. Reservation-attribute profiles are configured separately and can be used by the system's reservation logic.

Serial tracking & recalls

Use serial-tracking inquiries to trace serialized stock and recall menus to find and manage inventory associated with a recall process.

Work management05 / 13

Keep forward work supplied and prioritized.

This area connects replenishment setup with executable tasks. Methods define the available replenishment approach; requests and the task queue show work as it is raised, assigned, prioritized, and completed.

Replenishment methods

Maintain replenishment methods and their associated setup. These are configuration menus, separate from the requests that operators execute.

Replenishment requests

Review requests, their priority and status, and the inventory movement needed to replenish forward-pick work.

Task queue & task monitoring

View tasks by work type and status, follow assignment and progress, and use task dashboards to monitor activity across warehouse areas.

Priority work & exceptions

Surface high-priority or overdue work for review. The overview dashboard groups past-due picks, receipts awaiting putaway, replenishment requests, and open count tasks.

Inventory accuracy06 / 13

Plan counts and review discrepancies.

Cycle counting gives teams a controlled workflow for validating system inventory against a physical count, tracking the work, and reviewing differences before they are resolved.

Count plans

Organize count work for selected inventory or locations and provide the plan from which count tasks are worked.

Count tasks

Assign and track count activities, due status, and completion as part of the wider warehouse task workflow.

Variance review

Bring count differences forward for review and follow-up instead of treating a count as a simple quantity lookup.

Count dashboards

Monitor open counts, work due during a shift, and accuracy indicators through the count and performance views.

Value-added operations07 / 13

Manage assembly work inside the warehouse.

The kitting menus represent value-added warehouse work: define the kit structure, create assembly or disassembly work orders, carry out work at configured stations, and monitor progress.

Bills of material

Maintain the bill-of-material records that describe the components used by a kit and referenced by its work orders.

Assembly & disassembly work orders

Create and follow planned work to assemble a kit or disassemble one back into component handling.

Production stations

Configure the stations where kitting work is performed, then use those stations as the work moves through production.

Production monitoring

Review work-order progress and station activity so in-process work can be followed through completion.

Flow-through operations08 / 13

Connect inbound goods to outbound demand.

Cross-docking is presented as its own operational workbench rather than another storage-location screen. It focuses on coordinating eligible inbound product with outbound requirements for flow-through handling.

Cross-Dock Workbench

Open the dedicated workspace for reviewing and coordinating cross-dock activity.

Inbound-to-outbound coordination

Follow the relationship between eligible inbound goods and outbound demand as cross-dock work is planned and handled.

Configurable decision logic09 / 13

Shape the logic that directs warehouse work.

TK WMS places strategy configuration beside day-to-day execution. These menus are for implementation and operations teams maintaining how the system should make configured warehouse decisions—not for viewing a live transaction queue.

Strategy Engine

Maintain strategies used by configured warehouse processes. The menu provides a central place to work with strategy definitions rather than hard-coding every decision into a screen.

Strategy actions

Review the actions associated with strategy and rule behavior. Actions define what the system is configured to do when a decision path applies.

Suggested Location Rules

Configure the rules used to guide suggested warehouse locations in supported workflows, connecting location setup with the decision logic used during execution.

Reservation Attribute Profiles

Define attribute profiles used by inventory reservation controls, adding a configurable layer to how stock can be selected for warehouse demand.

Rules & policies10 / 13

Make warehouse policies explicit and manageable.

Rules & Profiles collects rule setup, actions, workflow policies, and reservation-related profiles. It is a configuration surface for authorized teams; the public guide explains the menu purpose without exposing rule values or customer configuration.

Business Rules Assistant setup

Open the assistant setup area associated with business-rule configuration and the policies that guide warehouse processes.

Rule Actions

Maintain the actions linked to configured business rules, providing the operational outcome side of rule-driven behavior.

Workflow Policies

Review and configure policies associated with workflow execution, keeping policy setup distinct from the screens and menus operators use.

Profiles & attributes

Manage reservation-attribute profiles used by reservation logic, in conjunction with strategy and inventory configuration.

Configurable user experience11 / 13

Design how warehouse users interact with work.

The system exposes designers and menu setup for configuring the experience around warehouse tasks. These are implementation tools alongside the workflows they help present.

Screen Designer

Configure the screens used in supported warehouse workflows, including the information presented as users perform their tasks.

Workflow Designer & Policies

Work with workflow structure and its policies so process steps and the rules governing them can be managed in the configuration area.

RF Menus

Maintain radio-frequency menu paths used by warehouse workers on handheld devices, separate from the public product site's navigation.

Voice configuration

Review voice-related options for guided warehouse tasks as part of the configurable worker experience.

Dashboards & inquiries12 / 13

Follow performance and investigate activity.

Dashboards summarize work by warehouse function; queries provide lookup paths into operational records and history. Together they help users move from an area-level view to a specific shipment, receipt, transaction, serial, or user activity inquiry.

Named dashboard views

Menu views include Putaway, Picking, Allocation, Replenishment, Inbound Performance, Task Activity, Consolidation, and Picking Performance dashboards.

Inbound & putaway

Follow inbound performance and putaway work, including receipts waiting for their next warehouse action.

Picking, allocation & consolidation

Review work at the planning and fulfillment stages, including picking performance and consolidation activity.

Queries & traceability

Use shipment, inventory, receipt, and transaction queries, plus serial-tracking and user-activity inquiries to investigate specific history.

Implementation & administration13 / 13

Give teams tools to configure and support the system.

These menus serve implementation, integration, administration, and training tasks around the operational WMS. They include both design-time tools and access/setup controls.

Report, Label & Interface Designers

Use separate designer menus to maintain report layouts, warehouse labels, and system-interface definitions.

Integration Sync & data utilities

Review integration synchronization, use import/export utilities for supported data workflows, and explore the API playground for API-oriented work.

Warehouse, user & access administration

Configure warehouse and business-unit records, user accounts, roles, access permissions, and the menus presented to users.

OUR GOAL

Give warehouse teams more clarity and control.

The product goal is to give warehouse teams a consistent path from configured setup to daily execution and back to review. TK WMS places task-oriented work beside the strategy, rules, and inquiry tools authorized teams use to shape and inspect the operation. This describes the product's intent, not a promise of measured business results.

01 / EXECUTE

Keep work connected

Warehouse activity is represented as related steps rather than isolated screens: receipt work leads to putaway and inventory, while available stock supports replenishment and order fulfillment.

02 / CONFIGURE

Make the logic manageable

Strategy, rules, workflow policies, and screen or RF/voice designers are exposed as setup areas for authorized teams, alongside the tasks those settings support.

03 / UNDERSTAND

See work at more than one level

Dashboards summarize work by function; query menus provide a path to investigate specific shipment, receipt, inventory, transaction, serial, or user-activity records.

Beyond day-to-day execution

The controls behind the operation.

These menus are notable because TK WMS exposes configuration surfaces beside execution: named strategy and rule tools, location and reservation controls, screen and RF/voice designers, and implementation utilities. They are setup tools for authorized teams, not claims that every process is automatically configurable.

01

Strategy Engine & actions

TK WMS exposes strategy definitions and strategy actions as configurable menus. Authorized teams can maintain decision logic used by configured warehouse processes instead of treating strategy as a hidden, fixed screen.

02

Suggested Location Rules & Reservation Attributes

Separate menus cover rules for suggested locations and profiles for reservation attributes. They address distinct decisions: where work may be directed and which inventory attributes matter during reservation.

03

Workflow, RF, and voice design

Screen Designer, Workflow Designer, Workflow Policies, RF menu setup, and voice-related options expose the worker-facing process as a configurable area. The public site describes these tools without revealing a customer's workflow settings.

04

Business Rules Assistant & replenishment methods

Business-rule setup, Rule Actions, and the Business Rules Assistant appear alongside replenishment-method configuration, putting rule maintenance and repeatable warehouse-work setup in the same configuration story.

05

Implementation tools and training

Report, Label, and Interface Designers sit alongside Integration Sync, import/export, and an API playground. Administration includes warehouse, business-unit, user, role, access, and menu setup, plus Training Phases 1–6.
