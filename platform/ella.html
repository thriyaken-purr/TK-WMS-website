TK WMS / MEET ELLA

A little guidance. A clearer next step.

Ella helps visitors understand basic TK WMS features and find the right product page.

Ask a basic product question

Visitors can type questions such as “What does the Inventory Workbench show?” or “What does the Strategy Engine do?” Ella matches basic questions to saved descriptions of TK WMS capabilities and replies in a compact messaging window.

Get an answer tied to the guide

Ella’s answers come from the product information stored in this website. A reply can link to a focused guide about inventory, receiving, strategy, or another documented topic. Visitors can use that link to continue reading beyond the short answer.

Know when a question needs more detail

Detailed configuration requests, unsupported features, and unfamiliar questions receive a polite fallback. Ella explains that she cannot answer exactly and points to a relevant page instead of inventing instructions or product claims.

Keep the product guide separate from live work

Ella uses saved replies and local topic matching. She does not use a connected AI service or access live inventory, orders, customers, or user accounts. The WMS’s development with AI and Ella’s current website behavior are distinct parts of the story.
