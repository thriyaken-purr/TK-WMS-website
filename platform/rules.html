Business rules & policies

Give configured behavior a clear home.

Review the distinct roles of business-rule setup, rule actions, and workflow policies.

Start with business-rule setup

TK WMS includes Business Rules Assistant setup as part of its configuration surface. This area is associated with the policies and rules guiding warehouse processes. It is intended for authorized configuration work, separate from daily task execution.

Maintain the outcome side of a rule

Rule Actions provide the action side of configured business-rule behavior. Rules and actions should be reviewed in the context of the supported warehouse process. The public guide explains their purpose without exposing warehouse-specific rule values.

Distinguish policy from screen design

Workflow Policies relate to execution policy. Screen Designer and Workflow Designer address the worker-facing process and structure. Policy setup and screen configuration are connected concerns, but they are maintained through different tools and should not be treated as interchangeable.
