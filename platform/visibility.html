Dashboards & operational queries

See the summary. Investigate the record.

Choose the right TK WMS view for functional monitoring or a specific operational inquiry.

Review warehouse functions

Named dashboards include Putaway, Picking, Allocation, Replenishment, Inbound Performance, Task Activity, Consolidation, and Picking Performance. Each provides a functional view rather than assuming one dashboard answers every operational question.

Investigate individual records

Shipment, inventory, receipt, and transaction queries provide lookup paths for specific records and history. Serial-tracking and user-activity inquiries serve additional traceability needs. An inquiry is a different tool from a functional performance view.

Connect overview with follow-up

Review an area-level dashboard when work needs attention, then choose an inquiry appropriate to the record being investigated. Available data and menus depend on authorization and warehouse configuration. The public website and Ella do not expose these operational records.
