TK WMS / VOICE & RECORDING

Voice belongs in the workflow.

Voice recording is part of the TK WMS story, alongside the voice-related options documented in its workflow configuration.

Bring voice into the product conversation

Voice recording is part of TK WMS. Voice is part of how the product’s interaction story is being developed, alongside the screens and menus used to present warehouse work.

Understand the surrounding workflow tools

The documented TK WMS configuration areas include Screen Designer, Workflow Designer, Workflow Policies, RF Menus, and voice-related options. These are different parts of the worker-facing experience: screen presentation, process structure, handheld navigation, and voice configuration.

Keep recording and execution distinct

Recording a voice input and completing warehouse work are separate activities. A recording on its own should not be understood as confirmation that inventory moved, an order shipped, or a task finished. The meaning of a voice interaction depends on the workflow in which it is used.

Review voice in its warehouse context

When exploring a voice-enabled experience, start with the task and the user’s role. Then review the available screen, RF, and voice options for that configured workflow. The workflow guide explains the surrounding design tools; the operations guides explain the warehouse work itself.
